Read this before you download

No warranty. Threadscope measures and suggests. You run it at your own risk, and the author accepts no liability for anything that happens to your system.

The script changes nothing on its own — run it bare and it only prints. Nothing deletes files, stops services, or installs anything. The two system settings it can change reset on reboot, and --revert undoes them immediately.

If it helped — do something kind for someone who'll never know it was you. That's the whole price.
